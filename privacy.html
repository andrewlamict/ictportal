ICT Portal – Privacy Policy

ICT Portal is a free study website for HKDSE ICT students, run by a teacher. Last updated: November 2026.

What we collect

If you sign in with Google, we store your Google name, email address, profile photo link, your XP and level, which pages and map spots you have completed, number of visits and last visit time. If you do not sign in (guest mode), progress is stored only in your own browser.

Why

To save your progress across devices and to let your teacher see class activity. Your data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything else.

Who can see it

Only you and the teacher administering this site. Data is stored with Google Firebase (Firestore).

Your choices

You can use the site as a guest without signing in. To have your data deleted, ask your teacher.
