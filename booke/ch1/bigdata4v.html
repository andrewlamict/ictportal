Big data has 4 Vs

Big data = huge, varied, fast and uncertain data that is hard to process with ordinary database systems. Learn the 4Vs, then sort the falling data cards.

V
VolumeHow much data. The data keeps growing and must be stored.
V
VarietyMany formats: audio, image, video, text, location. Different sources must be integrated.
V
VelocityHow fast data is generated and must be analysed.
V
VeracityHow accurate: uncertainty, accuracy and quality of the data.

How to play: each card falls towards the red line. Tap the V it shows (or press 1 2 3 4). You have 5 lives. 16 cards per round.
